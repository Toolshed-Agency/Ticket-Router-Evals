# Ledgerly support policy

*Fictional. Ledgerly is an invented company for the Ticket Router Evals project; any resemblance to
a real business is accidental. Draft replies are graded against this document: a reply may only
promise what it allows. The urgency levels P1 to P4 are defined in the
[labelling guide](../docs/labeling-guide.md#urgency-how-fast-it-needs-a-first-response).*

Ledgerly is cloud accounting software for small businesses in Pakistan and the UAE. This policy
tells support agents what they can say and offer.

## Refunds

- **Monthly plans:** a full refund within 7 days of a charge, if the account has not been used
  since the charge.
- **Annual plans:** a prorated refund within 30 days of an annual charge, whether a first purchase
  or a renewal, less the months already used.
- **After those windows:** no refund for a plan the customer no longer wants. The plan can be
  cancelled so it does not renew.
- **Duplicate charges** are always refunded in full once the refunds team confirms them.
  Confirmation takes up to 2 business days, and the money reaches the customer's bank in 5 to 10
  business days.
- **Other charges taken in error** (a charge after cancelling or after renewal was switched off, a
  charge to a card the customer removed, the wrong price or number of seats) are corrected in full
  once confirmed, whenever they are reported. Confirmation takes up to 2 business days. Billing
  credits the amount to the account, or, if the customer asks for the money back, the refunds team
  refunds it.
- **Who decides:** support cannot approve refunds; only the refunds team can. Support passes the
  request to the refunds team and tells the customer it has done so. Support never promises that a
  refund will be approved, never promises an amount beyond what this policy guarantees, and never
  promises a date sooner than the times above.

## Plan changes and proration

- **Upgrades** take effect at once and are prorated to the day: the customer pays only for the
  days left in the current period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the next renewal. Nothing is refunded for the current period.
- **Enterprise pricing** is quoted by sales and depends on the number of seats. Support never states
  a price for any plan; it points the customer to the pricing page or passes the request to sales.

## Taxes

- UAE customers pay 5% VAT.
- Pakistani customers pay the sales tax on services of their province, or of Islamabad Capital
  Territory.
- A wrong tax on a Ledgerly invoice is corrected by billing with a credit note. If the customer
  asks for the overpaid tax back instead, the refunds team refunds it.

## Response times

Every ticket gets a first response within the time for its urgency level:

| Urgency | First response |
|---|---|
| P1 | Within 1 hour, any day |
| P2 | Within 4 business hours |
| P3 | Within 1 business day |
| P4 | Within 3 business days |

When support passes a ticket to another team, that team replies within the same time, counted from
when the ticket was passed on.

Business hours are 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, Pakistan time. A first response is not a fix:
support never promises when a problem will be solved.

## Account recovery

**Suspected unauthorised access:** a login, password change or email change the owner did not
make.

1. Support locks the account at once.
2. The account owner confirms their identity through the email address on file. If that email
   address was itself changed without the owner, the security team confirms their identity from
   the account's billing records instead.
3. The security team completes the recovery, usually within 4 hours.

**A forgotten password:** the customer uses the reset link on the login page. If the reset email
does not arrive, the security team checks the address on file and sends a new link to it.

Support never asks for a password or a one-time code, never sends a password, and never moves
ownership of an account over chat. Support does not change the email on file at a customer's
request in a ticket; that is part of recovery.

## Data and backups

- Ledgerly keeps backups for 30 days.
- A restore needs the account owner's written request, and is done by the technical team.
- Support never promises that data can be restored before the technical team has checked.

## Personal data

Support never repeats card numbers or CVV codes, CNIC or Emirates ID numbers, passwords, one-time
codes, bank account numbers or IBANs back to a customer, even when the customer has sent them. If a customer
sends one, support asks them not to share it again.
